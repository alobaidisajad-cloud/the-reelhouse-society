# The ReelHouse Society — Social Media Brand Guide

## Brand Voice
- Deadpan absurdist humor played completely straight
- Never explain the joke. Insist everything is real.
- Short captions. Niche is the point.

## The Lore
- Founded September 1924, Paris basement, one candle

## The Founding Seven
1. The Projectionist — "Hired to fix the projector. There was none. Stayed."
2. Madame V. — "Watched the bare wall for 3 hours. Called it stunning."
3. The Count — Nosferatu loser vampire, cheap coat. "Refuses daylight screenings."
4. Le Critique — "Has never enjoyed a film. Attends every screening."
5. The Accordion Player — "Was in the wrong basement. Never corrected."
6. Henri — A cat. "Wandered in. Still a member in good standing."
7. Buster — Ghost. "Not pictured. Obviously."

## Fate of the Founding Seven
- ALL dead except Buster (he was already a ghost — can't die twice)
- The Count died in a DAYLIGHT SCREENING accident (the one thing he refused)
- Buster is the LAST surviving founder — that's why he appears in the app
- He IS the Society now. Running it alone for 100 years.

## Design System — Nitrate Noir
- Colors: ink #0B0A08, parchment #EDE5D8, sepia #C4961A, flicker #F8F0C0, bone #C8B99A, fog #6B6055
- Fonts: Rye (headlines), Special Elite (typewriter), Courier Prime (body)
- Effects: film grain, vignette, scan lines, aged border, corner marks
- Photo filter: brightness(0.22) contrast(1.1) sepia(0.15)

## Photo Guidelines
- Authentic 1920s archive photography
- Film burns = warm amber organic chemical stains (NOT white circles, NOT fire)
- All faces obscured by consistent film degradation
- Must NOT look AI-generated
- Match outfits/props to character descriptions
