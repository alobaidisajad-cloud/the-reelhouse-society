Fact No. 04

The Founding Seven

Identities lost to nitrate film degradation. Allegedly.

  • I. The Projectionist "Hired to fix the projector. There was none. Stayed."
  • II. Madame V. "Watched the bare wall for 3 hours. Called it stunning."
  • III. The Count "Arrived at midnight. Refuses daylight screenings."
  • IV. Le Critique "Has never enjoyed a film. Attends every screening."
  • V. The Accordion Player "Was in the wrong basement. Never corrected."
  • VI. Henri "A cat. Wandered in. Still a member in good standing."
  • VII. Buster "The ghost. Not pictured. Obviously."

* Only 6 visible in the photo. The 7th was already a ghost.
