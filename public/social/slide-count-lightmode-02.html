In Memoriam

ReelHouse UI Brightness Constraints.

  • I. Design Principle "ReelHouse will never feature a 'light mode'."
  • II. The Incident "In 1938, 'The Count' attended a daylight screening. He immediately oxidized into ash."
  • III. The Mandate "We honor his sacrifice by keeping our UI brightness at an absolute minimum."

* Note: We still sweep up his ash on occasion.
