The ReelHouse Society · The mascot, final design

Buster

An old sheet with two holes cut in it, and something in the dark looking out. Buster Keaton's hat, worn every night since 1924. One patch, where the armrest of seat F9 wore through. He is not a ghost. He is a permanent resident.

I · Alive

He floats. He sways. He blinks. Sometimes he looks away.

  • The draught. The hem strips move as if a door were open somewhere in an old cinema.
  • The drift. Every so often his brass eyes slide to one side, as if something in the room caught his attention, and slide back. Nothing else moves.
  • The blink. The brass goes out for a moment, and comes back.
  • The float. His shadow on the floor tightens as he rises, and softens as he settles.
II · The whole sheet acts

Six moods

Not only the eyes and the hat: suspicious, he leans in; moved, he slumps and the hem hangs heavy; proud, he stands taller; dimmed, he sinks and folds in; startled, the sheet puffs out.
