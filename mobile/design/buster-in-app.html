The ReelHouse Society · Buster, as the phone draws him

Buster, in the app

This page uses only what the app's drawing library can draw. No browser effects: the worn cloth, the felt, the soft light and the shaky pen line are all built into the drawing itself. Every size is shown at its real size in points, on the app's own backgrounds. This is what the phone will show.

I · On the house's black

Alive

  • He floats, and his shadow tightens as he rises.
  • He sways a little, like cloth hanging in still air.
  • The hem moves in a draught, strip by strip.
  • He blinks, and now and then his eyes drift to one side and back.
  • With Reduce Motion on, he simply stays still.
II · True size

At the sizes he's used today

From 56 points up he is the full drawing. Below that the fine detail would turn to mud, so he becomes the small drawing: the same character in fewer, bolder lines, with the hat, the holes, the brass, the three lines and the patch all kept.
