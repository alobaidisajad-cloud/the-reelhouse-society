Five Decisions for the Dispatch

Three of these are polish. Two are product, and both turned out to be already designed and never wired up — the desk with FILM, COVER and SERIES exists in your codebase and no screen mounts it. Where a plate below is a real component it says so; where it is a sketch, it says that too.

ONE

The preview should be the page

Today the preview sets your essay in a different typeface, size and colour from the reader — and drops the initial. You write, you preview, you file, and you get something else.

Cost · small · one screen, no new design

TWO

The rank, on the byline

Your film feed prints ✦ ARCHIVIST and ★ AUTEUR. The Dispatch prints neither — an auteur's name is tinted crimson and an archivist gets nothing at all. A rank should read loudest on the page it is sold for.

Three ways, so the choice is a choice. The byline already truncates a long name, so the mark alone costs almost no width; the word costs more and says more.

Cost · small · a sketch, needs your pick

THREE

The draft should say so

Drafts save on a debounce and flush again when the app backgrounds, so a four-thousand-word essay survives being killed. You are never told any of it happened, and a restored draft simply appears.

Cost · small · a sketch, needs your pick

FOUR

A dossier cannot carry its film

A take, a seeking and a wire can all attach a film. The dossier — the longest form, the one Auteur is sold for — cannot. The reader already draws a film credit and a cover backdrop for it, and the store already accepts them.

The desk on the left is not a sketch. DossierDesk is built, has FILM, COVER and SERIES, and no screen mounts it. This is wiring, not designing.

Cost · medium · the desk exists, the store accepts it

FIVE

What a film and a series buy the reader

The same essay, twice. On the left is every dossier the app can file today. On the right is the same one with the two things nothing can set.

Nothing in the app writes seriesId — it is read in four places and written in none. So the series page, the "Part II of four" line and NEXT IN THE SERIES are all built, tested, drawn, and unreachable.

Cost · largest · unlocks three things already built

Every plate is rendered from the components in your repository. The ones marked Real component or Built, unmounted are the app's own code shown where it would go; the two marked Sketch are drawn in the generator and exist nowhere else, which is why they need a decision rather than a review.
